## Rules

- **Build something new.** Fresh repo after 11:00 AM. Only work done today is judged.
- **Teams of 4**, assigned from the team form.
- **Ship a working demo.** A live product on real input, not slides.

This repo (donna) was created at 11:23 AM on Nov 22, so it qualifies as the fresh project repo. The notes were moved here from the earlier `hackathon` repo.

## Submission

Due by **3:00 PM** through the submission form (linked in the Hacker Package PDF). One submission per team, containing:

1. The project link
2. A 2-minute demo video that anyone can open
3. Every partner tool used (this is what makes the project eligible for Partner Picks)

## Judging

Stay at your table. Each team pitches twice, to two different judges: 3 minutes of demo, then 2 minutes of questions. Lead with the demo. The top 4 teams then pitch live to partner CEOs.

| Weight | Criterion | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 25% | Technical execution | It works, live, on real input |
| 25% | Use of ElevenLabs and partner tools | Central to the product, not bolted on |
| 25% | Usefulness and impact | A real problem for a clear user |
| 15% | Demo and communication | Clear in 3 minutes |
| 10% | Creativity | Something judges remember |

## Prizes

**Grand Prize**, picked by partner CEOs from the top 4 live pitches:

- The project is sent to the organizers' YC partners
- A pitch meeting with Front Row Ventures
- 3 months of ElevenLabs Scale per member

**Partner Picks.** The best projects built with each startup's tool go straight to that startup's founders.

| Prize | Award |
|---|---|
| Best Use of ElevenLabs | 3 months of ElevenLabs Pro for every team member |
| Best agents on Maritime (top 3) | 3 months of Maritime Growth, plus $100 / $50 / $50 in LLM credits |
| Best project built with Codevisor | Mac mini |
| Best project built with Wasp | $100 Wasp swag pack, a 30-minute call with Wasp's CEO, and a feature on Open SaaS |

The Grand Prize winner cannot also win Best Use of ElevenLabs. Partner prizes stack with anything.

Also mentioned in earlier emails: 1:1 founder chats with most sponsors for the winner of that sponsor's category, and follow-up from Waterloo Venture Group and Front Row Ventures for the top teams.

## How admission worked

1. Register on Luma (approval required).
2. Call the organizers' voice agent at +1 (249) 481-2091 from the registered phone number: a 4-minute chat about you and what you want to build, used to match people with teams and companies. Seven of the top 10 applicants had taken the call.
3. Acceptances were rolling until Friday, Nov 20.
4. Fill out the RSVP + team signup form. The final deadline was 7 PM on Nov 21, with a warning that anyone who had not filled it in would not be let in.

A "First 40 acceptances" guest list (names with a one-line "known for") was shared as a PDF on Nov 19.

## Where the sources disagree

- **End time.** Luma and the emails say 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM. The Hacker Package schedule ends with awards at 5:25 PM, and hacking itself is only 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
- **Mac mini.** The Nov 19 email calls it an "M6 Mac mini" in the prize pool. The Hacker Package lists a "Mac Mini" for Best project built with Codevisor, without a model.
- **Founder chats.** The Nov 19 email promises 1:1 founder chats with most sponsors for category winners. The Hacker Package names only four partner prizes, and only Wasp's includes a call.
